# MicroUs

MicroUs is a tool aimed at streamlining scheduling and roster management for gamers. As we all know, balancing real-life responsibilities with gaming sessions makes for a gigantic headache. Multiplayer games often rely on having people gather to play at a certain time, and much of the time it comes down to someone to herd everyone together. For some large-scale events, such as raids in an MMO, there comes also a necessity to organize and allocate players based not only on their schedule but also on their suitability to the group (i.e. skill level,item level and roles). 

So while you're there trying to herd everyone to play at a certain time, everyone else is sitting around saying "Micro us!"

## Usage (conceptually)
-**Input availability** People submit their usernames, day & hours free, and optional data depending on the content (you don't need to know how skilled someone is if you're just playing friendslop... right?)
-**Schedule analysis** Using the acquired data, evaluate the best possible timeslots to actually game. Additionally have functionality to organize groups based on overall skill/ilvl/roles if it's for an mmo.
-**Output schedules** Present organized and clear schedules for you and everyone to be able to reference from. Additional functionality to integrate this into discord via bot messages are also a possibility.
